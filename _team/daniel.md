Daniel Woods was a Student Researcher at the PINPON lab during the summer of 2026.
His work focused on developing automated market makers (AMMs) on Sui blockchain for peer-to-peer energy trading using Sui Move.
In this study, a comprehensive comparison between Sui and Ethereum was conducted, providing reseachers and practitioners with insightful references.
