Michel Justinen was a Student Researcher at the PINPON lab during the summer of 2025 and 2026 supported by NSERC USRA.
His work focused on developing automated market makers (AMMs) for peer-to-peer energy trading.
In particular, he implemented three types of AMMs and combined them to develop a hybrid pricing curve. In the improved AMM, energy price regulation and more practical considerations were integrated into the system.
