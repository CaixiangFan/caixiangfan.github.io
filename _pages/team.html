The Privacy-Preserving and INtelligent P2P Operations Network (PINPON) Lab is a research group at The King's University focused on developing trustworthy, intelligent, and decentralized computing systems for real-world applications.

Our research brings together blockchain, artificial intelligence, privacy-preserving computing, and peer-to-peer (P2P) networks. We are particularly interested in how these technologies can work together to build systems that are secure, transparent, intelligent, and privacy-preserving.

Our current research includes:

  • Blockchain and P2P Systems — decentralized applications, smart contracts, and peer-to-peer systems.
  • P2P Energy Trading — blockchain-based energy markets, automated market makers (AMMs), distributed energy resources, and renewable energy integration.
  • Privacy-Preserving AI — zero-knowledge machine learning (ZKML), federated learning, and privacy-preserving design.
  • Intelligent Decentralized Systems — multi-agent reinforcement learning for decentralized coordination and decision-making.
  • Tokenization and Digital Assets — blockchain-based tokenization of energy, infrastructure, renewable energy certificates, carbon assets, and other real-world assets.

We welcome students and collaborators who are interested in exploring the intersection of computing, AI, blockchain, privacy, and sustainable technologies.
